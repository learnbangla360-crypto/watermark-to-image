Step-by-step guide to watermarking an image

Add a text watermark

Leave Text selected and type your line, such as a name, a website or a copyright notice. Choose a color. White with the soft shadow switched on is readable on most photos. Dark text works on light backgrounds like snow or paper.

Add a logo watermark

Switch to Logo image and choose a file. A PNG or WebP with a transparent background looks best. A logo with a white box around it will show that box. If yours has one, Background Remover can clear it first.

Set size, opacity and rotation

Size is a percentage of the image width. Opacity runs from 5% to 100%, and 30 to 50% is a good range for a mark that's visible but doesn't fight the photo. Rotation from -90 to 90 degrees helps with diagonal stamps.

Choose a position

Pick center, a corner, or the top or bottom middle. Corner marks keep the subject clean. Center and Tile across image are harder to remove.

Save the file

Choose PNG for lossless output, JPG for smaller photos, or WebP for small files on the web. Click Add Watermark & Download. The preview is a scaled copy, and the saved file is made at full size.

Expert tips and common mistakes

  • Don't make it too faint. If it's barely visible on your phone, it's useless.
  • Don't make it too loud. A watermark that ruins the picture gets the photo ignored.
  • Put the mark over detail, not plain sky. Smooth areas are easy to retouch out.
  • Use tile mode for valuable shots. A single corner stamp can be cropped off.
  • Keep the clean original. You can't remove a watermark from the saved copy.
  • Compress after stamping. A saved PNG can be big, so run it through Compress Image File before sharing.

A watermark discourages casual copying, but it doesn't stop a determined person, and it isn't a copyright registration. It's a visible credit.

Troubleshooting

  • "That file is not a supported image." Use JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP.
  • HEIC photo won't open. Convert it first with HEIC to JPG Converter.
  • Nothing shows on the preview. Type some text or choose a logo, and check that opacity isn't at the minimum.
  • White text vanishes on a bright photo. Switch the color to a dark one or leave the shadow on.
  • The logo has a white box. Use a PNG with a transparent background.
  • The saved file is huge. Choose JPG or WebP, or lower the quality slider.
  • The tab is slow on a big photo. Close other tabs, or resize the image first.

Glossary

Opacity
How solid the watermark is. 100% is solid, and lower values show more of the photo.
Tiling
Repeating the watermark in a pattern across the whole image.
Alpha channel
The part of a PNG that stores transparency.
Canvas
The browser feature used to draw the image and the watermark.
EXIF
Hidden camera data in a photo, such as date, device and orientation.

Frequently asked questions

Your image is processed in your browser and is never uploaded. The page draws your photo and watermark on a canvas on your own device and makes no network requests while you work. That suits unreleased work and client photos, though you should avoid shared computers.

No. Open the page, drop in an image, add your text or logo and download the result. There's no signup, no email prompt and no software to install. It works in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and mobile.

Can I use my own logo as a watermark?

Yes. Switch the type to Logo image and choose a file. A PNG or WebP with a transparent background looks best, because a logo with a solid white box will show that box on your photo. You can also set its size, opacity, rotation and position.

What's the best opacity for a watermark?

Between 30% and 50% works for most photos. The mark stays readable without covering the picture. Go higher on busy, detailed images where a faint mark disappears, and lower when you want a subtle credit on a clean background. Check the live preview before you save.

How do I stop someone cropping out my watermark?

Use Tile across image, which repeats the mark over the whole picture, or place it over the subject instead of a corner. A watermark discourages casual reuse, but it can't stop a determined person from editing it out. Keep your clean original.

Will adding a watermark reduce my image quality?

PNG output is lossless, so the photo itself isn't degraded. JPG and WebP are lossy, and the quality slider controls how much. The saved file keeps your original pixel dimensions unless it's above 48 megapixels, when it's scaled down to fit browser limits.

Can I watermark several images at once?

Not in this version. The tool handles one image at a time, so you'd repeat the steps for each file. Your text, logo and settings stay in place while you use Change image, which makes a series of similar photos quicker.

Can I add a watermark to a photo on my phone?

Yes. Tap the drop area, choose a photo from your gallery or files, and use the same sliders as on desktop. The preview updates as you drag. If a large photo is slow on an older phone, resize it first, then add your watermark.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the page has finished loading. The tool needs no network connection while you work, so you can switch to airplane mode and still watermark a photo you already have. If you closed the tab, reload the page when you're back online.
